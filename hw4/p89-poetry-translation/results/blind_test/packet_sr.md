## B001 · T12 鹊桥仙·纤云弄巧

**原文**

```text
纤云弄巧，飞星传恨，银汉迢迢暗度。金风玉露一相逢，便胜却人间无数。柔情似水，佳期如梦，忍顾鹊桥归路。两情若是久长时，又岂在朝朝暮暮。
```

**译文**

```text
Fine clouds show their skill;
flying stars convey their grief.
Across the far Silver River, they secretly cross.
Once the golden wind and jade dew meet,
they surpass countless meetings in the mortal world.

Tender feelings are like water;
the happy tryst is like a dream.
How can they bear to look back on the homeward road over the Magpie Bridge?
If two hearts love enduringly,
what need is there for morning and evening together?

Notes
Silver River: the Milky Way.
Golden wind and jade dew: conventional images of autumn, alluding to the lovers’ annual meeting.
Magpie Bridge: in Chinese legend, the bridge formed by magpies across the Milky Way for the Cowherd and Weaver Girl’s yearly reunion.
```

## B002 · T09 枫桥夜泊

**原文**

```text
月落乌啼霜满天，江枫渔火对愁眠。姑苏城外寒山寺，夜半钟声到客船。
```

**译文**

```text
The moon sets, crows cry, frost fills the sky;
River maples and fishing-boat lights face a sorrowful sleep.
Beyond the walls of Suzhou, Hanshan Temple—
At midnight, its bell reaches the traveler’s boat.

Notes
- Suzhou (Gusu): the ancient city near which the poem is set.
- Hanshan Temple: a temple near Suzhou, famed for its midnight bell.
- Fishing-boat lights: lights on fishing boats.
- “Face a sorrowful sleep”: the Chinese phrase 对愁眠 is deliberately ambiguous; it may suggest sleeping face-to-face with sorrow, lying awake facing the scene, or the scene keeping company with a sorrowful sleeper.
```

## B004 · T11 虞美人·春花秋月何时了

**原文**

```text

```

**译文**

```text
When will the spring flowers and autumn moon ever end?
How much of the past do I remember?
Last night the east wind blew again through the little tower;

The carved balustrades and jade-white steps should still be there,
Only the rosy faces have changed.
If you ask how much sorrow I can hold,
It is like a river of spring water flowing east.
```
