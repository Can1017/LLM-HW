## B001 · T04 夜雨寄北

**原文**

```text
君问归期未有期，巴山夜雨涨秋池。何当共剪西窗烛，却话巴山夜雨时。
```

**译文**

```text
You ask when I’ll return—no date is set.
Night rain in the Ba Mountains swells the autumn pool.
When shall we trim the candlewick together by the west window,
And then talk of the time of night rain in the Ba Mountains?

Notes
Ba Mountains: a mountainous region in southwest China, here a remote place far from the friend in the north.
```

## B002 · T08 浣溪沙·一曲新词酒一杯

**原文**

```text
一曲新词酒一杯，去年天气旧亭台。夕阳西下几时回？无可奈何花落去，似曾相识燕归来。小园香径独徘徊。
```

**译文**

```text
A new song, a cup of wine;
Last year’s weather, the old pavilion and terrace.
The sun sinks in the west—when will it return?
Helpless, the blossoms fall away;
Like old acquaintances, the swallows come back.
Alone I pace the fragrant path of the small garden.
```

## B013 · T06 水调歌头·明月几时有（上片）

**原文**

```text
明月几时有？把酒问青天。不知天上宫阙，今夕是何年。我欲乘风归去，又恐琼楼玉宇，高处不胜寒。起舞弄清影，何似在人间。
```

**译文**

```text
When did the bright moon first appear?
Cup in hand, I ask the blue sky.
I do not know, in the heavenly palaces,
what year this evening belongs to.
I wish to ride the wind and return there,
yet fear those jade towers and jeweled halls—
so high, I could not bear the cold.
I rise and dance, playing with my clear shadow:
how does that compare with the world of men?
```


**译文**

```text

Night rain in the Ba Mountains swells the autumn pond.

Notes

2. Autumn pond: the pond is swollen by the night rain.
```
